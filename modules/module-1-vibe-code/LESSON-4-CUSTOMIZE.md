# Lesson 4: Customize

**Time:** 20 minutes | **You'll do:** Tweak colors, copy, and sections until it feels right

---

## What Happens in This Lesson

This is the iteration lesson. You'll go through each part of your site and make it genuinely yours — not just technically functional, but something you're proud of.

Claude handles all the code. You just describe what you want.

---

## How to Give Good Customization Feedback

The key is specificity. The more specific your feedback, the faster you get what you want.

**Content changes** (what the site says):
- "Change my tagline to: [exact words]"
- "Rewrite the About section bio to: [exact words]"
- "Add a Projects section with these 3 projects: [project 1], [project 2], [project 3]"

**Visual changes** (how it looks):
- "Change the primary color to [color name or #hex code]"
- "Make the hero section taller with more padding"
- "Change the font to something more modern — try Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans"
- "Add a subtle background gradient to the hero section"

**Structure changes** (how it's organized):
- "Move the Contact section to before the About section"
- "Remove the Skills section — I don't need it"
- "Add a thin horizontal line between each section"

---

## Exercise 1: Content Pass (8 min)

Go through every section of your site and make sure the content is exactly right.

Check each section:

**Hero / Header:**
- Is your name how you want it displayed?
- Is the tagline your exact words?
- Are your social links correct?

**About:**
- Is the bio in YOUR voice? Does it sound like you?
- Is it the right length?
- Anything missing or wrong?

**Work / Projects / Skills:**
- Are all the entries accurate?
- Any missing?
- Any that shouldn't be there?

**Contact:**
- Is your email correct?
- Are your social handles correct?

Tell Claude every change you want. Wait for it to update. Refresh your browser (Cmd+R or F5) and check again.

---

## Exercise 2: Visual Pass (8 min)

Now make it look the way YOU want.

Go through:

**Colors:**
- Does the color palette feel right?
- Want it darker? Lighter? More colorful? More minimal?
- Try: "Change the background to #[hex]" or "Make the accent color more vibrant"

**Typography:**
- Does the font feel right?
- Try: "Change to a different Google Font — something clean and modern"
- Try: "Make the headings bigger" or "Increase the body text size to 18px"

**Spacing:**
- Does it feel cramped or too spread out?
- Try: "Add more whitespace between sections" or "Make the hero section taller"

**Mobile:**
- Resize your browser window to phone width. Does it still look good?
- Tell Claude if anything breaks: "On mobile the navigation overlaps the hero — fix it"

---

## Exercise 3: One Extra Thing (4 min)

Add one element that makes your site a little more interesting:

**Easy options:**
- A hover effect on your links (they change color when you mouse over them)
- A subtle animation when the page loads
- A profile photo (paste the image path and Claude will add it)
- A "Currently" section — what you're focused on right now
- A footer with copyright and the current year

Pick one. Ask Claude to add it.

---

## Quick Reference

```
Refresh browser:  Cmd+R (Mac) / F5 (Windows/Linux)
Good feedback:    Specific — "Change X to Y"
Content changes:  Tell Claude the exact words you want
Visual changes:   Describe the effect or give a color/size
Structure:        "Move X before Y" / "Remove X" / "Add X"
```
