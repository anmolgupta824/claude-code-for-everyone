# Lesson 2: Design Brief

**Time:** 15 minutes | **You'll do:** Tell Claude exactly what you want — so the first build is close to right

---

## What Happens in This Lesson

You'll write a design brief — a document that tells Claude everything it needs to know to build your website. The better your brief, the less back-and-forth in Lesson 3.

This is the director's job. You don't need to know how to code. You just need to know what you want.

---

## What Goes in a Design Brief

A design brief answers 4 questions:

1. **Who are you?** — Name, title, 1-2 sentences about what you do
2. **What sections does your site have?** — Think of these like pages within a single page
3. **What does it look like?** — Colors, fonts, style, examples
4. **What should visitors feel or do?** — The purpose of the site

---

## Exercise 1: Answer the Design Questions (8 min)

Work through these questions. Type your answers directly — no need for full sentences.

### About You

- **Your name:** [First name, last name, or handle]
- **Your tagline:** [One sentence — what you do or who you are. Examples: "Product designer building tools for creators" / "Freelance writer covering tech and culture" / "Student, builder, and chronic overthinker"]
- **Your bio:** [2-4 sentences about you. Write it like you'd write a Twitter bio — honest, specific, a little personality]

### Sections

Which sections do you want on your site? Pick from the list below or add your own:

- [ ] Hero / Header (your name + tagline — almost everyone has this)
- [ ] About (longer bio, background, what drives you)
- [ ] Work / Projects (things you've built, written, or done)
- [ ] Skills (what you're good at)
- [ ] Writing / Blog (if you publish content)
- [ ] Contact (email, social, or a contact form)
- [ ] Resume / CV (link to download)
- [ ] Now (what you're focused on right now — inspired by nownownow.com)
- [ ] Custom: [your idea]

You don't need more than 4-5 sections. Keep it simple.

### Visual Style

- **Color palette:** [Describe in plain language: "clean white and navy", "dark background with bright yellow accents", "earth tones — tan, green, brown", "minimal grayscale", or paste the hex codes if you know them]
- **Font vibe:** [Modern and clean / Classic serif / Bold and strong / Minimal and airy]
- **Overall feel:** [Professional / Creative / Personal / Technical / Warm / Bold]
- **Any sites you like the look of?** [Optional but helpful — just the URL or site name]

### Purpose

- **Who will visit this site?** [Recruiters / Potential clients / Friends and family / Anyone curious / Other]
- **What do you want them to do?** [Email you / Follow you on social / Read your work / Hire you / Just know you exist]

---

## Exercise 2: Write the Brief (5 min)

Now write it as one cohesive document. Create `design-brief.md` in your `~/my-website` folder:

```markdown
# My Website Design Brief

## About Me
- **Name:** [Your name]
- **Tagline:** [One sentence]
- **Bio:** [2-4 sentences]

## Sections (in order)
1. Hero — name + tagline
2. [Section 2]
3. [Section 3]
4. [Section 4]
5. Contact

## Visual Style
- **Colors:** [description or hex codes]
- **Font vibe:** [your preference]
- **Overall feel:** [1-2 words]

## Purpose
- **Audience:** [who will visit]
- **Goal:** [what you want them to do]

## Anything Else
[Any specific requests, things you definitely want or definitely don't want]
```

Fill it in with your actual answers. Save it.
